BRIGHT TECH SOLUTIONS - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

1. WORKING HOURS
Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Employees get a one-hour lunch break between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM. Friday prayer break is from 1:00 PM to 2:30 PM.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE
Every full-time employee gets 20 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave must be requested at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal. Up to 5 unused leave days can be carried forward to the next year.

3. SICK LEAVE
Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. For sick leave longer than 2 days, a medical certificate is required.

4. REMOTE WORK
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with their manager's approval. Remote workers must be available on Slack during office hours.

5. SALARY AND PAYROLL
Salaries are paid on the 1st working day of every month by bank transfer. Annual salary reviews take place every July.

6. EXPENSES
Travel and work expenses are reimbursed within 15 days. Receipts must be submitted through the finance portal. Expenses above 20,000 PKR need prior approval from the department head.

7. IT AND EQUIPMENT
Each employee receives a company laptop. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours. Installing unlicensed software is not allowed.

8. CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees must treat colleagues and clients with respect. Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited and should be reported to HR immediately. All complaints are handled confidentially.
